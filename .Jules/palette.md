## 2024-10-02 - Form Labels and Icon Button Accessibility in Templates
**Learning:** Discovered a consistent pattern in the app's HTML templates (`admin_login.html`, `admin.html`) where `<label>` elements visually positioned next to inputs lacked explicit `for`/`id` bindings, preventing proper screen reader association. Additionally, icon-only buttons (like the "▶" Run Scraper buttons) lacked accessible `aria-label`s, rendering them ambiguous to assistive technologies.
**Action:** When adding new forms or interactive elements in this app's templates, strictly ensure all `<label>`s have explicit `for` bindings matching the input's `id`, and always supply descriptive `aria-label`s (along with `aria-hidden="true"` on the visual icon) for buttons that rely solely on iconography.
